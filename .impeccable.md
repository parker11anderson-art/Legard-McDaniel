## Design Context

### Users
- **Primary:** East Valley homeowners (Mesa 85204 and surrounding — lots of 1970s–90s housing stock), often 40–70, on a phone, frequently mid-problem: a breaker that won't reset, dead outlets, flickering lights, an old panel an inspector or insurer flagged. Job to be done: confirm this is a real, licensed, local outfit and call them.
- **Secondary:** property managers, small-commercial owners and GCs needing tenant improvements, new-build electrical, or ongoing maintenance. Job: confirm commercial capability and licensing, then call.
- Conversion = a phone call during shop hours (Mon–Fri 6–4). Everything routes to the phone number.

### Brand Personality
- Three words: **steady, exacting, plainspoken.**
- Voice: a foreman who has done this for forty years and doesn't need to oversell. Short sentences. Specific nouns (panels, breakers, conduit), not adjectives.
- Emotional goal: relief and confidence — "these people have been here forever and will do it right."

### Aesthetic Direction
- **Light theme.** Daylight phone use by homeowners; warm desert-paper surface, deep ultramarine ink, a single copper accent (the color of the wire itself).
- **Brand mark:** the company's existing "LM" monogram (slanted strokes with thin inline stripes, ultramarine `oklch(0.36 0.19 272)` / `#1e1a96`), redrawn as vector in `src/components/Monogram.astro` and `public/logo-monogram*.svg`. The hand-lettered script wordmark from the original is replaced on the web by Archivo italic, which echoes the monogram's slant. Dark sections and the footer use the brand blue; copper is reserved for calls to action and "live current".
- **Motif: the electrician's own documents.** Equipment rating plate (company facts), panel schedule/directory (services list), one-line schematic (process + service area), drafted line illustrations instead of stock photos.
- Local signal: the Superstition Mountains ridgeline on the eastern horizon of the East Valley.
- Type: Archivo variable (width + weight axes — expanded heavy for display, like a stamped nameplate; condensed for labels) paired with Public Sans for body (the U.S. government typeface — plain, official, highly legible; Atkinson Hyperlegible was rejected because its slashed zeros made "200A" read as "2ØØA").
- **Anti-references:** template trade sites with a stock hard-hat guy, lightning-bolt clip art, yellow/black hazard palette, trust-badge soup, icon-in-a-rounded-square grids, dark mode with neon "electric" glow.

### Design Principles
1. **The phone number is the product.** Visible in the first viewport, sticky on mobile, repeated at every decision point.
2. **Show the trade, don't decorate.** Every visual is a document or drawing an electrician would actually recognize.
3. **Specific over superlative.** License number, address, hours, year founded — facts beat "best in the Valley."
4. **One accent, used rarely.** Copper marks calls-to-action and live "current"; nothing else.
5. **Clean finish.** The past complaint was mess left behind — the site states the clean-up standard plainly.
